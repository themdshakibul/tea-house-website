# Tea House Website

## 🌿 Overview

Tea House is a modern and minimal landing page for a tea and beverage
brand. The website showcases featured tea products, customer reviews,
brand story, and latest news & events.

## 🏠 Sections Included

### 🔹 Hero Section

-   Catchy headline: **"It's good tea time at The Tea House"**
-   Short description about tea and botanical solutions\
-   Call-to-action button: **Explore More**
-   Visual display of tea drinks with a 5.00 Trust Pilot rating

### 🔹 Featured Products

Showcased tea items: - Milk Tea - Black Tea - Lemon Tea - Green Tea

Each product includes an image and short description.

### 🔹 About the Brand

Section titled **"Great Tea, Freshly Presented"** with: - Brand story -
Unique taste description - Premium quality highlight

### 🔹 Testimonials

Section: **"Meet Our Super Clients"** - Displays customer reviews -
Highlighted testimonial from Ilham Yuda (Businessman) - Button: **Show
All**

### 🔹 News & Events

Recent posts include: - Collecting 8 points for discount (Feb 05,
2027) - Blog images related to tea and lifestyle

### 🔹 Footer

Includes: - Quick Links (Home, About Us, Insurance, Privacy Policy) -
Services (Life, Car, Health, House Insurance) - Help (FAQs, Contact
Us) - Newsletter subscription field - Social media icons

## 🛠️ Tech Suggestion (Optional)
